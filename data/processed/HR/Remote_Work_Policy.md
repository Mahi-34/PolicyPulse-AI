**1. Purpose**

This policy defines eligibility, approval, working arrangements, attendance, equipment, security, and responsibilities for hybrid and remote work.

**2. Scope**

This policy applies to employees whose roles and business requirements permit remote or hybrid work.

**3. Eligibility**

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval, role requirements, team coordination, and business needs.

**4. Work Arrangements**

Remote work may be used as part of an approved hybrid arrangement. Employees are expected to maintain agreed working hours, availability, communication, and performance standards.

**5. Approval**

Employees must obtain manager approval for planned remote-work days. Managers may decline or modify remote-work arrangements where business requirements, collaboration needs, customer commitments, or operational conditions require on-site presence.

**6. Permanent Fully Remote Arrangements**

This policy does not define a final approving authority for permanent fully remote arrangements. Such arrangements therefore require separate organizational guidance or approval outside the definitive rules stated in this document.

**7. Working Hours and Attendance**

Employees working remotely must remain available during agreed working hours and follow attendance and meeting requirements.

**8. Workspace and Equipment**

Employees must maintain a suitable work environment and use company-approved devices and equipment where required.

**9. Information Security**

Remote work must use company-approved devices, secure authentication, approved network/security controls, and other requirements defined by IT Security Policy and IT Standards.

**10. Responsibilities**

Employees maintain availability, security, and work standards. Managers evaluate business suitability and approve routine remote-work days. HR maintains policy guidance. IT defines and supports applicable technical security controls.

**11. Exceptions**

Exceptions may be considered for documented business or employee circumstances. Permanent arrangements are not governed by a final authority in this policy.

**12. Revision History**

2025 — Initial hybrid work guidance issued. 2026 — Remote-work eligibility, security requirements, and exception handling updated.
