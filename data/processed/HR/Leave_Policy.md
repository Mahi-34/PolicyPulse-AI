## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes leave entitlements, application procedures, approval levels, attendance expectations, extended leave requirements, and responsibilities for employees of NovaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees unless a separate employment arrangement or applicable statutory requirement provides a different entitlement.

## 3. Leave Categories

The standard leave categories are Annual/Earned Leave, Casual Leave, Sick Leave, and Bereavement Leave.

## 4. Annual Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 18 days of Annual/Earned Leave per applicable leave year.

## 5. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 8 days of Casual Leave per applicable leave year, subject to business requirements and approval.

## 6. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of Sick Leave per applicable leave year. Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when absence is unplanned.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

Employees may receive 5 days of Bereavement Leave for qualifying circumstances.

## 8. Leave Application and Approval

Planned leave should be requested through the designated process before the absence. Approval depends on the number of consecutive leave days:

| Consecutive Leave   | Required Approval    |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Up to 3 days        | Manager              |
| 4-10 days           | Department Head      |
| More than 10 days   | HR + Department Head |

## 9. Extended Leave

Leave exceeding 10 consecutive days requires HR and Department Head approval. Supporting information may be requested where applicable.

## 10. Unplanned Absence

Employees must notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible. Repeated or unexplained absence may be reviewed under applicable attendance procedures.

## 11. Critical Projects

Managers should consider operational continuity and critical project requirements when reviewing planned leave. Business constraints do not remove the need for a fair and documented review.

## 12. Responsibilities

Employees submit accurate leave requests and communicate unplanned absence. Managers review requests and business continuity. HR maintains policy guidance and employee records.

## 13. Exceptions

Exceptions may be considered for documented circumstances and applicable statutory or organizational requirements.
