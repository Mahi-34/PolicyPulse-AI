**1. Purpose**

This policy establishes information security and access-control requirements for organizational accounts, authentication, remote access, provisioning, modification, revocation, and incident reporting.

**2. Scope**

This policy applies to employees, contractors where applicable, organizational systems, accounts, devices, and information resources.

**3. Account Provisioning**

IT provisions organizational access after receiving an authorized request containing employee ID, department, role, required systems, and manager approval.

**4. Authentication**

Organizational accounts must use approved authentication controls. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required where supported and designated by IT standards.

**5. Passwords and Credentials**

Users must protect credentials, use approved password practices, and never share authentication credentials with other individuals.

**6. Access Modification**

When an employee changes role or responsibilities, access privileges must be reviewed and modified according to the employee's new business requirements.

**7. Access Revocation**

During offboarding, IT disables organizational system access by the employee's final working day, subject to documented security or operational requirements.

**8. Remote Access**

Remote work must use company-approved devices, secure authentication, MFA where required, and approved network/security controls. Organizational credentials must not be shared.

**9. Security Incidents**

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT Service Desk or designated IT security channel as soon as reasonably possible. IT coordinates technical investigation and containment.

**10. Employee Responsibilities**

Employees must protect organizational information, follow security controls, report incidents, and use systems only for authorized purposes.

**11. Manager and HR Responsibilities**

Managers provide accurate role and authorization information. HR provides relevant employee lifecycle information. IT manages provisioning, modification, revocation, and technical security controls.

**12. Exceptions**

Exceptions require documented justification, risk consideration, and applicable IT/security approval.

**13. Revision History**

2025 — Initial policy issued. 2026 — Access lifecycle, MFA, remote access, and incident reporting requirements updated.
