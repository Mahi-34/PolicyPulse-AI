## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for assignment, registration, use, transfer, return, recovery, and lifecycle management of NovaCore Technologies IT assets.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors where applicable, IT personnel, managers, and organizational IT assets.

## 3. Asset Categories

Covered assets include laptops, desktops, mobile devices, tablets, network equipment, peripherals, and other IT equipment assigned or controlled by the organization.

## 4. Asset Allocation

IT assigns assets based on approved role and business requirements. Asset assignment must be recorded in the applicable asset register and acknowledged by the recipient.

## 5. Employee Responsibility

Employees must protect assigned assets, use them for authorized business purposes, follow security requirements, and report loss, theft, or damage promptly.

## 6. Asset Registration

IT maintains records including asset identifier, asset type, assigned employee, department, assignment date, status, and relevant lifecycle information.

## 7. Role Change and Transfer

When an employee changes role or department, the manager should notify HR and IT. IT reviews assigned assets and determines whether equipment, configuration, or ownership records require modification.

## 8. Lost or Damaged Assets

Loss, theft, or material damage must be reported promptly to the manager and IT. IT records the incident and coordinates recovery, replacement, or further review as applicable.

## 9. Offboarding and Asset Return

Employees must return assigned IT assets by the final working day unless an approved exception applies. IT records returned assets and coordinates recovery of organizational equipment.

## 10. Recovery and Reassignment

Returned assets are inspected, recorded, securely handled, and may be reassigned, repaired, retired, or disposed of according to applicable organizational requirements.

## 11. Responsibilities

IT manages asset records and lifecycle controls. Managers communicate employee and role changes. Employees safeguard assets and complete required returns. HR supports lifecycle information.

## 12. Exceptions

Exceptions to asset return or assignment requirements require documented business justification and applicable approval.

## 13. Revision History

2025 - Initial policy issued. 2026 - Asset lifecycle, role-change, and offboarding controls updated.
