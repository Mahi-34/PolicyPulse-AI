**1. Purpose**

These guidelines define legal due diligence, documentation, contract review, compliance, data protection, intellectual property, single-source considerations, and vendor compliance expectations.

**2. Scope**

The guidelines apply to vendors and prospective vendors whose relationships require legal or compliance review.

**3. Legal Due Diligence**

Legal review may consider vendor identity, ownership, regulatory status, contractual history, data handling, intellectual property, conflicts of interest, and other relevant risks.

**4. Required Vendor Documentation**

Depending on the vendor category, relevant documentation may include registration records, tax information, certifications, compliance declarations, ownership details, data-protection information, and contract documentation.

**5. Contract Review**

Non-standard vendor contracts require Legal review. Legal evaluates material terms, risk allocation, mandatory clauses, applicable laws, data protection, intellectual property, liability, termination, and dispute provisions.

**6. Data Protection**

Contracts involving organizational or personal data should address appropriate data protection obligations, security expectations, permitted use, incident handling, and relevant contractual protections.

**7. Intellectual Property**

Vendor arrangements involving software, content, technology, or deliverables should clearly address ownership or permitted use of intellectual property.

**8. Compliance**

Vendor relationships should be screened for relevant regulatory obligations, conflicts of interest, required certifications, sanctions or compliance considerations where applicable, and other material legal risks.

**9. Single-Source Procurement**

Single-source procurement may be appropriate for proprietary technology, specialized services, an only-qualified vendor, or other documented circumstances. The business justification and applicable approvals must be recorded.

**10. High-Risk Vendors**

High-risk vendors may require enhanced legal or compliance review before activation or contracting.

**11. Compliance Issues and Suspension**

Legal may recommend suspension of a vendor where material legal or compliance concerns exist. Procurement may initiate a suspension review. These guidelines do not define a final approving authority for vendor suspension.

**12. Responsibilities**

Business owners provide accurate vendor context. Procurement manages commercial onboarding. Finance performs applicable financial verification. Legal performs legal and compliance review and provides recommendations.

**13. Exceptions**

Exceptions must be documented and assessed for legal and compliance impact before approval.

**14. Revision History**

2025 — Initial guidelines issued. 2026 — Legal due diligence, single-source, data protection, and vendor compliance guidance updated.
