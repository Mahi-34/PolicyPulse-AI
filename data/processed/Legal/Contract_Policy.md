## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for contract initiation, legal review, approval, execution, mandatory clauses, exceptions, and contract record retention.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to vendor, software, consulting, service, confidentiality, data protection, and other business contracts entered into by NovaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 3. Contract Types

Contracts may include vendor agreements, software licenses, consulting agreements, service agreements, NDAs, DPAs, and other business arrangements.

## 4. Contract Initiation

The business owner identifies the requirement, commercial context, counterparty, scope, estimated value, and relevant risk considerations. Procurement participates where the contract relates to sourcing or vendor selection.

## 5. Legal Review

All non-standard vendor contracts require Legal review. A standard approved template without material changes may follow the applicable simplified process, subject to other required approvals.

## 6. Contract Value and Review

Contract value affects approval and review requirements as follows:

| Contract Value / Type                   | Legal Review                      | Financial / Management Approval      |
|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Up to n 5,00,000 standard process       | As required by contract type/risk | Applicable business/Finance approval |
| n 5,00,001 - n 25,00,000                | Required                          | Finance approval as applicable       |
| Above n 25,00,000                       | Required                          | Finance approval + senior approval   |
| Non-standard vendor contract, any value | Required                          | Applicable business/Finance approval |

## 7. Mandatory Clauses

Where applicable, contracts should address confidentiality, data protection, intellectual property, liability, termination, dispute resolution, and governing law.

## 8. Approval

Contracts must receive applicable business, financial, Legal, and management approvals before execution. Approval requirements may apply cumulatively where multiple controls are triggered.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions require documented justification and appropriate Legal/business approval. An exception to a Legal review step does not automatically waive financial, procurement, security, or other requirements.

## 10. Execution

Only authorized organizational signatories may execute contracts on behalf of NovaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 11. Record Retention

Executed contracts, approvals, material drafts, legal comments where required, and related records must be retained according to organizational retention requirements.

## 12. Responsibilities

Business owners define requirements and commercial context. Procurement manages applicable sourcing processes. Finance performs financial review. Legal performs legal review and advises on contractual risk. Authorized signatories execute contracts.

## 13. Revision History

2025 - Initial policy issued. 2026 - Contract thresholds, mandatory clauses, and review workflow updated.
