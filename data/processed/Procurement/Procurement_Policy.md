## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standards for purchasing goods and services, vendor selection, quotation requirements, purchase orders, approvals, exceptions, and emergency procurement.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to departments, employees, Procurement personnel, budget owners, and vendors participating in purchases made on behalf of NovaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 3. Definitions

Procurement Committee means the designated cross-functional body responsible for reviewing purchases above the applicable Procurement threshold. Single-source procurement means procurement from one identified supplier without a competitive quotation process.

## 4. Procurement Principles

Procurement decisions must be transparent, documented, commercially reasonable, and consistent with approved business requirements. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Applicable Finance, Legal, security, and business controls remain relevant.

## 5. Purchase Requisition

A purchase request should identify the business requirement, estimated value, specifications or scope, budget owner, requested delivery date, and any known supplier or urgency considerations.

## 6. Vendor Selection

Procurement evaluates suppliers using relevant commercial, technical, quality, compliance, delivery, and risk criteria. Higher-risk or specialized vendors may require additional due diligence and Legal review.

## 7. Quotation Requirements

Quotation requirements are based on purchase value and are summarized below:

| Purchase Value          | Minimum Quotation Requirement   |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Up to n 1,00,000        | 1 quotation                     |
| n 1,00,001 - n 5,00,000 | 2 quotations                    |
| Above n 5,00,000        | 3 quotations                    |

## 8. Procurement Approval

Purchases exceeding n 5,00,000 require Procurement Committee approval before a purchase order is issued. Purchases above n 10,00,000 also remain subject to applicable Finance approval, including CFO approval under the Finance policy.

## 9. Purchase Orders

A purchase order should be issued before a supplier becomes contractually or financially committed, except where an approved emergency or documented exception applies.

## 10. Single-Source Exception

Single-source procurement may be permitted for proprietary technology, specialized services, an only-qualified supplier, or another documented business justification. The requesting department must document the rationale and obtain applicable approvals.

## 11. Emergency Procurement

Emergency procurement may be used where delay could materially affect business continuity, safety, critical operations, or preventable loss. The reason, supplier selection, commercial basis, and retrospective approvals must be documented within 2 business days.

## 12. Responsibilities

Requesting departments define requirements and business justification. Procurement manages sourcing and commercial evaluation. Finance performs applicable financial review. Legal performs applicable legal review. Approvers confirm that required controls have been completed.

## 13. Exceptions

Exceptions require documented justification and approval from the applicable authority. An exception to competitive quotations does not automatically remove Finance, Legal, security, or contractual requirements.

## 14. Record Retention

Procurement requests, quotations, evaluations, approvals, purchase orders, exceptions, and related records must be retained for 7 years unless a longer period is required.

## 15. Revision History
