**1. Purpose**

This procedure defines the steps and responsibilities for registering, evaluating, approving, activating, reviewing, and suspending vendors.

**2. Scope**

It applies to new vendors providing goods or services to NovaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**3. Vendor Classification**

Vendors may be classified as standard, strategic, high-risk, technology, or single-source based on business importance, risk, specialization, and sourcing conditions.

**4. Vendor Registration**

The requesting department initiates the vendor request. Procurement records the vendor's legal name, address, tax identification details, bank information, ownership information, contact details, certifications, and relevant declarations.

**5. Required Documents**

Typical documentation includes registration certificates, tax records, bank details, ownership information, certifications, compliance declarations, conflict-of-interest declarations, and other documents required for the vendor category.

**6. Due Diligence and Compliance Screening**

Procurement performs applicable commercial and onboarding checks. Compliance concerns must be documented and escalated for appropriate review.

**7. Finance Verification**

Finance verifies applicable financial information, banking details, tax information, and other financial controls before vendor activation.

**8. Legal Review**

Legal review is required where the vendor relationship, contract, risk profile, data handling, intellectual property, regulatory exposure, or other circumstances make legal review applicable.

**9. Vendor Approval and Activation**

The workflow is: vendor identified → registration → document collection → compliance screening → Finance verification → Legal review where required → Procurement approval → vendor activation.

**10. Periodic Review**

Strategic, high-risk, technology, and other designated vendors may be subject to periodic review of documentation, performance, compliance, and risk.

**11. Vendor Suspension**

Procurement may initiate a suspension review when material compliance, performance, documentation, or risk concerns arise. Legal may recommend suspension where legal or compliance concerns exist. This procedure does not define a final approving authority for vendor suspension.

**12. Responsibilities**

Requesting departments provide business information. Procurement manages onboarding and records. Finance performs applicable financial verification. Legal performs applicable legal review. Relevant stakeholders provide timely documentation.

**13. Exceptions**

Exceptions to standard onboarding requirements must be documented and approved through the applicable departmental process.

**14. Revision History**

2025 — Initial procedure issued. 2026 — Vendor classification, compliance screening, and onboarding workflow updated.
