EXPENSE POLICY**

**1. Purpose**

This policy defines eligible employee expenses, reimbursement limits, approval requirements, supporting documentation, and reimbursement procedures.

**2. Scope**

The policy applies to employees incurring legitimate business expenses on behalf of NovaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**3. General Principles**

Expenses must be business-related, reasonable, documented, within applicable limits, and submitted within the required timeline.

**4. Expense Categories and Limits**

| **Expense Category**   | **Maximum Reimbursement Limit**   | **Notes**                                         |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Hotel                  | ₹6,000 per night                  | Business travel; supporting invoice required      |
| Meals                  | ₹1,500 per day                    | Business travel or approved business activity     |
| Local Transport        | ₹1,000 per day                    | Business-related local travel                     |
| Client Entertainment   | ₹3,000 per person                 | Business purpose and participant details required |
| Miscellaneous          | ₹5,000 per claim                  | Must be business-related and justified            |

**5. Approval Requirements**

Claims up to ₹25,000 require manager approval. Claims from ₹25,001 to ₹1,00,000 require Department Head approval. Claims above ₹1,00,000 require Finance Controller approval.

**6. Submission Deadline**

Employees must submit claims within 15 calendar days of the expense date.

**7. Supporting Documentation**

Valid invoices, receipts, travel records, participant details where applicable, and business justification must accompany claims.

**8. Reimbursement Process**

The employee submits the claim; the designated approver reviews it; Finance validates compliance; approved claims are processed.

**9. Non-Reimbursable Expenses**

Personal expenses, unsupported expenses, expenses outside limits without an authorized exception, penalties or fines, and unrelated expenses are not reimbursable.

**10. Exceptions**

Exceptional expenses may be considered with documented business justification and appropriate approval.

**11. Responsibilities**

Employees submit accurate claims. Managers and Department Heads review business necessity. Finance validates compliance and reimbursement eligibility.

**12. Record Retention**

Expense claims and supporting records must be retained for 7 years unless a longer period is required.

**13. Revision History**

2025 — Initial policy issued. 2026 — Expense limits, approval levels, and emergency handling updated.
