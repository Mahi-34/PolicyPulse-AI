## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the process for identifying, classifying, responding to, escalating, resolving, closing, and documenting operational incidents and service requests.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to business operations teams, department managers, IT teams involved in technical incidents, and employees reporting operational issues.

## 3. Operational Activities

Operations coordinates business service requests, operational incidents, service-impacting issues, escalation, stakeholder communication, and closure documentation.

## 4. Service Requests

Requests should be recorded with requester information, business requirement, priority where relevant, requested date, and responsible owner.

## 5. Incident Identification and Reporting

An incident is an event that disrupts or threatens normal business operations. Employees should report incidents through the designated organizational channel with sufficient information for classification and response.

## 6. Incident Classification

Incidents are classified as P1 Critical, P2 High, or P3 Normal according to business impact, urgency, scope, and operational disruption.

| Priority      | Description                                          | Initial Response   |
|---------------|------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| P1 - Critical | Critical business disruption or major service impact | 30 minutes         |
| P2 - High     | Significant service or business impact               | 2 hours            |
| P3 - Normal   | Normal operational issue with limited impact         | 1 business day     |

## 7. Initial Response

Operations acknowledges and coordinates the incident according to priority. The initial response should establish impact, affected services or processes, known information, and immediate actions.

## 8. Escalation

Incidents are escalated according to priority and the applicable Escalation Matrix. Technology incidents may require escalation to IT for technical investigation.

## 9. Resolution

The responsible team investigates and performs corrective action. Operations coordinates business impact and stakeholder communication for operational incidents.

## 10. Closure

An incident may be closed after the responsible owner confirms that the service or process has been restored or the applicable resolution has been completed and closure information has been recorded.

## 11. Documentation

Incident records should include classification, timestamps, actions taken, escalation history, resolution details, and relevant stakeholder communication.

## 12. Responsibilities

Operations coordinates business incidents and operational impact. IT investigates technology incidents. Managers coordinate business decisions and stakeholder actions where required.

## 13. Exceptions

Exceptions may apply for force majeure, planned maintenance, restricted security communications, third-party failures, or other documented circumstances.
