**1. Purpose**

This policy defines service-level targets, incident classification, response and resolution expectations, communication, escalation, exceptions, monitoring, and reporting.

**2. Scope**

The policy applies to operational incidents and services managed or coordinated by Operations, including technology-related incidents where IT is the responsible technical team.

**3. Incident Classification**

P1 incidents are critical, P2 incidents are high impact, and P3 incidents are normal priority.

**4. Service Level Targets**

| **Priority**   | **Response Target**   | **Resolution Target**   |
|----------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|
| P1 — Critical  | 30 minutes            | 4 hours                 |
| P2 — High      | 2 hours               | 8 hours                 |
| P3 — Normal    | 1 business day        | 3 business days         |

**5. P1 Communication**

For P1 incidents, an initial update should be provided within 60 minutes. Subsequent updates should be provided at least every 2 hours until service restoration, subject to applicable security communication restrictions.

**6. Escalation**

Incidents are escalated according to priority, impact, elapsed response time, and the Escalation Matrix. Technology incidents may be escalated to IT for technical investigation.

**7. Resolution**

Resolution targets represent operational service-level objectives. Resolution may require coordination among Operations, IT, business departments, vendors, or other responsible teams.

**8. Monitoring**

Operations monitors response and resolution performance and records relevant timestamps, escalation events, exceptions, and outcomes.

**9. Exceptions**

SLA exceptions may include force majeure, planned maintenance, third-party failure, restricted security incident communications, or other documented circumstances.

**10. Responsibilities**

Operations coordinates service levels and business communication. IT handles technical investigation where applicable. Managers support business decisions and escalation.

**11. Reporting**

Periodic SLA reporting may include incident volumes, priority distribution, response performance, resolution performance, escalations, exceptions, and recurring issues.

**12. Revision History**

2025 — Initial policy issued. 2026 — SLA targets, communication requirements, and exception handling updated.
